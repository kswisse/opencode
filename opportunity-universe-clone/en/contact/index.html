General enquiries

Questions about the database, a listing that looks wrong, or a deadline that changed? Write to info@opportunity-universe.com and include the link to the programme you mean.

For organisations

Running a scholarship, fellowship or programme? Use the Submit Opportunity form in the header menu - listings are reviewed before they go live. For partnerships and promotion, email the same address with "Partnership" in the subject line.
