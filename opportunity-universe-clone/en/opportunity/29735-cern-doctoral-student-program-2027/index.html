CERN Doctoral Student Program 2027

CERN · Geneva, Switzerland · 21 days left

The CERN Doctoral Student Program 2027 lets selected PhD candidates carry out their research at CERN while remaining enrolled at their home university. Participants receive a monthly allowance, travel support and access to CERN's experiments and training programme.

What it covers

  • Monthly allowance for the duration of the placement
  • Travel support to and from Geneva
  • Supervision by an experienced CERN scientist

Who can apply

PhD students already enrolled at a university in physics, engineering or computing. Any nationality is welcome, provided the home-university supervisor confirms the research plan.

How to apply

Secure a supervisor at your home university, then submit the joint application through the CERN online form before the call closes.

This is a condensed summary. Deadlines, amounts and eligibility can change — always confirm on the official page.
