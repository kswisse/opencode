Caltech Scholarships 2027

California Institute of Technology (Caltech) · Pasadena, United States · 37 days left

The Caltech Scholarships 2027 support international students who want to join Caltech's undergraduate or doctoral programmes. Awards are need-aware and cover tuition plus a living stipend for the duration of the programme.

What it covers

  • Full tuition for the accepted programme
  • Annual living stipend
  • One-time travel allowance for students arriving from abroad

Who can apply

First-year undergraduate applicants and prospective PhD students holding a bachelor's degree in a STEM field. Non-US citizens are eligible; English proficiency proof is required.

How to apply

Apply through the standard Caltech admissions route and tick the funding section. Decisions follow the regular admission timeline.

This is a condensed summary. Deadlines, amounts and eligibility can change — always confirm on the official page.
