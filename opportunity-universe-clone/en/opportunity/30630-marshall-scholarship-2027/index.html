Marshall Scholarship 2027

Marshall Aid Commemoration Commission · United Kingdom · 4 days left

The Marshall Scholarship 2027 funds one or two years of postgraduate study in the United Kingdom for graduates with strong academic records and leadership potential. The award covers tuition, a living stipend and a travel grant.

What it covers

  • Tuition fees at any UK university
  • Monthly living stipend
  • Travel grant between the US and the UK

Who can apply

US citizens under 26 holding a bachelor's degree, with a record of academic achievement and leadership in their community.

How to apply

Submit the online application in the autumn, then attend an interview if shortlisted. Awards are announced after the interview round.

This is a condensed summary. Deadlines, amounts and eligibility can change — always confirm on the official page.
