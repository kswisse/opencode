OU Mobility Scholarship — Apply Now

Our own small grant for students on the move.

The OU Mobility Scholarship is a modest grant that helps students cover the travel and setup costs that stop otherwise funded moves from happening - flights, deposits and the first month in a new city.

Basics

  • Open to students worldwide with a confirmed place or programme offer
  • Covers travel and initial settling costs, not tuition
  • Rolling decisions four times a year
