How We Find Opportunities

Our pipeline, in plain language.

1. Wide crawl

An automated crawl sweeps university pages, government portals, foundation sites and the major opportunity platforms several times a day.

2. De-duplication

Entries are matched by programme name, provider and deadline so the same call never shows up twice under different titles.

3. Enrichment

Each record gets funding status, eligibility tags, field, country and a structured deadline before it reaches the site.

4. Review

Flagged or borderline entries are checked by a human before publishing. Anything that cannot be verified stays out.
