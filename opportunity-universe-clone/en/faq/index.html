Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we hear most often.

Is Opportunity Universe free?

Yes. Browsing, searching and the weekly newsletter cost nothing. An optional Pro membership adds saved-search alerts.

Do you run the scholarships you list?

No. We are a database. Applications go directly to the organisation running the programme.

How often is the database updated?

Continuously. New calls are picked up during the daily crawl and checked for duplicates before they appear.

Can I apply from my country?

Eligibility differs per programme. Use the country filter on any listing page to see only what accepts applicants from where you live.

How do I submit my own opportunity?

Use the Submit Opportunity form in the header menu. Listings are reviewed before they go live.
