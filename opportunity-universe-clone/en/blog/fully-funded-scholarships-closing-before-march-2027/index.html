Fully Funded Scholarships Closing Before March 2027

17 Sep 2026 · 5 min read

The spring cycle is when fully funded awards with March deadlines open. Planning now is the difference between a rushed application and a competitive one.

Master's awards

Chevening, Erasmus Mundus and national government awards all interview in spring. They need two recommendation letters, so ask referees in November.

Doctoral routes

PhD funding moves faster and earlier. Contact potential supervisors in December with a two-paragraph research pitch and your transcript.

Condensed summary written for this mirror. Dates and eligibility change every cycle — check the official sources.
