Paid international internships closing in October and November 2026

20 Sep 2026 · 3 min read

Paid internships with international deadlines cluster in the next eight weeks. Research, policy and engineering roles dominate this window, and most of them cover travel and housing on top of a stipend.

Where the money is

Government labs and research institutes pay the best stipends - NASA pathways, EU frame programmes and national science agencies. Private-sector internships often pay salary but rarely cover flights.

How to prepare

One CV per field, two referees warned in advance, and a two-week buffer before each deadline. Applications submitted on the final day fail more often for missing documents than for quality.
