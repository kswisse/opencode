Fully Funded Scholarships in Europe for Late 2026 and 2027

24 Sep 2026 · 4 min read

Europe still runs some of the most generous postgraduate funding on earth, and the biggest calls for the 2026-2027 cycle open between September and December. Three routes deserve attention if you want a fully funded master's or PhD.

United Kingdom: Chevening and Marshall

Chevening covers a one-year master's anywhere in the UK and expects two years of work experience; the Marshall Scholarship targets US graduates and funds one to two years of study. Both close in early autumn, so start the application at least six weeks before the deadline.

Switzerland: ETH and other doctoral routes

ETH Zurich recruits fully funded PhD candidates through individual professor pages rather than one central call. Watch chair pages from October; a supervisor willing to sponsor you matters more than a general application.

Germany: DAAD and foundation scholarships

DAAD and political foundations fund Master's and PhD study with stipends plus tuition. Language requirements vary - many programmes run entirely in English.

Condensed summary written for this mirror. Dates and eligibility change every cycle — check the official sources.
