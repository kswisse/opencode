About Us

Who we are and how this database came to be.

Opportunity Universe was built by a small team that kept missing deadlines because opportunities live scattered across platforms, embassy pages and mailing lists. We track them in one place so you can spend your time applying instead of searching.

What we do

  • Scan the open web and partner platforms every day for new programmes
  • De-duplicate entries so the same call never appears twice
  • Tag funding, eligibility and deadlines so filters stay trustworthy
  • Send one weekly digest matched to your country and field

What we do not do

We do not run the programmes we list. Applications always happen with the official provider - every card here links straight to their page.
